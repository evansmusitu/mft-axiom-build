# Axiom trust and claim governance

The governing rule is simple: implementation, measurement, evidence, independent validation, and broad claims are separate authorities.

Protected Git refs and sealed authorities do not move without explicit authorization. Exact-head CI evidence binds only the implementation and scope it tested. Failed evidence is retained. Degraded experiments remain `MEASUREMENT_ONLY_UNBUDGETED` and have neither budget nor claim authority.

The candidate cannot independently validate itself. An Evidence Observatory export is a review packet, not a review. Independent review must authenticate its own origin, identify the exact candidate and ledger root, disclose methodology and conflicts, retain failures, and sign or otherwise bind the review artifact through an approved external trust root.

No marketing seal, vendor logo, provider label, URL, checksum, or checked-in `attested=true` field can substitute for that process. Global or world-best claims remain prohibited until the Phase 15 external-comparison gate is genuinely satisfied.
