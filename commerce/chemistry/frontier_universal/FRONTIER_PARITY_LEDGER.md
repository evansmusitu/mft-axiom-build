# MUSITU Chemistry Frontier Parity Ledger

This ledger prevents the universal application work from deleting customer-requested capabilities that live outside the Android APK.

## Universal application core — required in every capable carrier

- 18-chapter Chemistry mastery engine and 36-mark diagnostic
- FSRS review, mastery, confidence, bookmarks, error routing, exam history and progress backup/restore
- 5/15/30/90-minute timed practice, Chemistry Tools and Final 48
- Free/Premium gating and six plan identities
- device-bound signed `MUSITU1` entitlement verification and offline Premium
- Diagnose -> Revise -> Prove onboarding
- Chemistry Rescue as a secondary accelerated loop, not the app identity
- Scientific Response OS / SRG v1: equation, ink, structure, mechanism, graph, apparatus, particle, argument, accessibility and graph-data
- certified expression boundary: no answer generation, auto-balancing, correctness hints, hidden retrieval, remote AI or client-side marking authority
- strict script CSP, allowlisted legacy action bridge, no unsafe-eval
- safe areas, focus-visible, reduced motion, contrast and coarse-pointer controls
- Android carrier plus installable/browser web/PWA carrier

## Hosted customer/trust layer — required product capability, not embedded authority

The following are deliberate customer-requested upgrades and must not be deleted merely because the study core is portable:

- `/chemistry/` — public product/storefront surface
- `/chemistry/app` — dedicated MUSITU Chemistry application identity
- `/chemistry/install` — platform-aware installation concierge
- `/chemistry/plans` — plan decision/recommendation surface with server-owned prices
- `/chemistry/verify` — release provenance and public verification center
- `/chemistry/support` — lifecycle support, recovery and activation guidance
- `/chemistry/privacy` — privacy and measurement controls
- `/chemistry/terms` — commercial/product terms
- `/chemistry/experience` — privacy-preserving aggregate field-experience evidence
- `/chemistry/rescue` — Rescue campaign/secondary learning route
- `/chemistry/manifest.webmanifest?v=2` and `/chemistry/sw.js` — installed app identity/offline carrier
- server-side checkout, settlement verification, licence issuance, seat claim and purchase recovery
- privacy-preserving field metrics with GPC/DNT suppression and minimum sample thresholds

The offline core may link to these services but must never duplicate payment or licence-signing authority.
