## Response A — "Interested / send more information"

Objective: QUALIFIED -> MEETING.

Reply structure:
1. Thank the named owner.
2. Send the external buyer package and the buyer-specific proof card.
3. State the exact workflow hypothesis in one sentence.
4. Offer two meeting slots.
5. Ask for a business/domain owner and technical/data owner to attend.

Commercial boundary:
Do not lead with pricing unless asked. The meeting should establish economic consequence and buyer authority first.

## Response B — "Can you demo it?"

Objective: QUALIFIED -> MEETING -> POV design.

Demo:
- 3 min buyer workflow + current baseline.
- 5 min live Axiom quantitative execution using approved/synthetic inputs.
- 3 min evidence, governance and failure retention.
- 2 min buyer-specific economic proof design.
- 2 min commercial decision path.

End with:
"If this clears the agreed success thresholds, which commercial path should we design toward: a paid proof, an enterprise deployment, or an OEM/strategic integration?"

Never demo unsupported operations or claim unverified global superiority.

## Response C — "Send pricing"

Objective: preserve value-based selling while making commercial anchors concrete.

State:
- Verified Proof of Value: USD 50,000 negotiation anchor.
- Founding Enterprise Deployment: USD 350,000 first-year contract-value anchor.
- OEM / Strategic Intelligence License: USD 1,000,000 minimum first-year commitment anchor.

Then say:
"These are proposed structures, not one-size-fits-all list prices. Final commercial terms depend on the workflow, deployment boundary, usage, support/security obligations and measured economic value."

Ask:
"Which of the three structures is closest to the decision you are evaluating?"

## Response D — "We only do unpaid pilots"

Objective: avoid indefinite free custom work while preserving a route forward.

Reply:
"We can support a tightly bounded no-fee technical validation only if it uses synthetic/non-sensitive inputs, has a short fixed scope, and has an agreed commercial decision gate before work begins. Buyer-specific integration, data onboarding, custom evaluation or sustained delivery belongs in a paid proof."

Required before any unpaid validation:
- named executive sponsor;
- defined workflow;
- fixed case count/timebox;
- success metrics;
- decision date;
- agreed post-validation commercial path.

No open-ended unpaid pilot.

## Response F — security/procurement request

Objective: QUALIFIED -> DILIGENCE without creating unsupported assurances.

Send:
- architecture/evidence sheet;
- submission/capability boundary;
- enterprise identity/RBAC evidence;
- data handling/privacy evidence available;
- security appendix template;
- procurement checklist;
- historical verification evidence with exact claim boundary.

Never invent:
- SOC/ISO certification;
- penetration-test outcome;
- uptime/SLA;
- insurance;
- data residency commitment;
- legal compliance attestation.

Missing evidence stays missing and becomes a diligence item.

## Meeting close — mandatory decision

A substantive working session must end with one explicit next state:

### Path 1 — paid POV
"Are you prepared to authorize the proposed USD 50,000 proof-of-value structure, subject to your procurement/legal process, so we can evidence this workflow against agreed success criteria?"

### Path 2 — enterprise
"If the proof criteria are already sufficiently demonstrated, who owns approval for the proposed USD 350,000 founding-enterprise path and what documents are required to enter procurement?"

### Path 3 — OEM / strategic
"If the integration and downstream economics are strategically relevant, can we open an OEM/strategic term-sheet process around a proposed USD 1,000,000 first-year minimum commitment?"

If none:
Record the exact blocker, owner, evidence required and decision date.
Do not mark the opportunity QUALIFIED merely because the meeting was pleasant.

## Capital-state discipline

SENT -> ROUTING -> QUALIFIED -> MEETING -> POV-PROPOSED -> POV-SIGNED -> ENTERPRISE-PROPOSED / OEM-NEGOTIATION -> LOI -> CONTRACTED -> INVOICED -> CASH

No state may be skipped by inference.
