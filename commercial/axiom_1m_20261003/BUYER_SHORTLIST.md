# Initial Buyer Shortlist — MUSITU Axiom Verified Intelligence Fabric

This is a prioritized outreach universe, not evidence that any organization intends to buy Axiom.

Selection criteria:
1. ability to support a six/seven-figure technology commitment;
2. high-value finance/risk/research/operational workflows;
3. current public evidence of AI/data/platform investment;
4. opportunity for enterprise deployment or downstream OEM distribution;
5. credible route to a measurable proof of value.

## Decision-maker route

For each target, seek one executive sponsor plus one technical/economic owner:
- CFO / Group Finance Director;
- Chief Risk Officer;
- CIO / CTO / Chief Data or AI Officer;
- Head of Strategy/Transformation;
- business-unit CEO;
- product/partnership EVP for OEM targets.

Avoid generic info@ outreach when a named executive or partnership function can be identified through lawful public channels.

## Qualification question

Do not spend a major sales cycle until the buyer can answer yes to at least three:
1. Does the target workflow cost or influence >10x the proposed contract value?
2. Is the workflow recurrent?
3. Is auditability/governance important?
4. Is there an executive owner?
5. Can a buyer-approved dataset/case be provided?
6. Is there budget authority or a defined procurement path?
7. Could the solution expand across teams/customers if the proof succeeds?
