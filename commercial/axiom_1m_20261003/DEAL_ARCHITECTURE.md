# MUSITU Axiom Deal Architecture

All amounts below are **non-binding negotiation anchors** for internal commercial strategy. They are not current product prices, executed offers or customer commitments. Final terms require authorized human/legal/commercial approval.

## Transaction 1 — Verified Proof of Value

**Anchor:** USD 50,000

Purpose:
- prove one high-value workflow using buyer-authorized data;
- establish baseline economics;
- measure cycle time, analyst effort, error/rework, scenario coverage and auditability;
- produce an evidence-bound go/no-go report.

Illustrative structure:
- 50% initiation payment;
- 50% on delivery of the agreed evidence pack;
- 2–4 week commercial scope, with delivery timing negotiated and legally approved;
- amount may be credited against an enterprise agreement if negotiated.

Do not promise any percentage ROI before measurement.

Immediate ask:
> Authorize a paid proof of value around one workflow and nominate an executive owner plus technical/data owner.

## Transaction 2 — Founding Enterprise Deployment

**Anchor:** USD 350,000 first-year contract value

Purpose:
- organization/workspace deployment;
- pooled usage;
- governed access;
- selected integrations;
- implementation;
- agreed evidence and support process;
- expansion across an agreed number of teams/workflows.

Illustrative commercial composition:
- USD 250,000 annual enterprise license/usage commitment;
- USD 100,000 implementation/integration package.

Exact seat count, pooled units, support, SLA, deployment model and payment schedule remain negotiated fields.

Immediate ask:
> Approve a founding-enterprise order form subject to the procurement checklist and negotiated SOW.

## Transaction 3 — OEM / Strategic Intelligence License

**Anchor:** USD 1,000,000 minimum first-year commitment

Purpose:
- embed Axiom capabilities into a partner platform or managed service;
- enable downstream customer workflows;
- preserve MUSITU ownership of Axiom core IP;
- permit negotiated branding/API/tenant/integration rights;
- create minimum commercial commitment plus expansion economics.

Illustrative composition:
- USD 500,000 minimum annual technology/usage commitment;
- USD 250,000 integration and launch program;
- USD 250,000 prepaid expansion capacity / reserved enterprise capability pool.

Alternative structure may use a minimum guarantee plus overage/revenue share.

Not included by default:
- transfer of Axiom source ownership;
- permanent exclusivity;
- unrestricted sublicensing;
- unrelated improvements;
- customer-specific SLA or indemnity;
- regulated decision delegation.

Immediate ask:
> Sign an LOI/term sheet for an OEM or strategic deployment with a USD 1M minimum first-year commercial commitment, subject to diligence and final definitive agreements.

## Negotiation guardrails

1. Preserve MUSITU ownership of Axiom and general improvements.
2. Time-box any exclusivity; scope it narrowly by geography/vertical/use case.
3. Price exclusivity separately.
4. Require a minimum commitment if downstream distribution rights are granted.
5. Separate implementation fees from recurring platform economics.
6. Define usage/overage instead of offering unlimited compute by default.
7. Make SLA, support, security and data obligations explicit negotiated terms.
8. Never activate enterprise entitlement before procurement evidence is complete.
9. Contracted value, invoiced value and cash received must be tracked separately.

## Seven-day capital accounting

Do not report "we made $1M" when only an unsigned proposal exists.

Track:
- qualified pipeline;
- verbal intent;
- LOI/term sheet value;
- signed contract value;
- invoiced value;
- cash collected.

The seven-day target should preferably be one of:
- signed $1M minimum commitment;
- two signed $500k-equivalent commitments;
- four signed $250k-equivalent commitments;
- or actual cash if a buyer's procurement/payment process permits it.
