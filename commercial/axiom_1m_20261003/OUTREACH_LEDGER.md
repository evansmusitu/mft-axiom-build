## Qualification rule on reply

A reply advances only if it produces at least one of:
- named executive/product/partnership owner;
- scheduled technical-commercial meeting;
- request for technical evidence/demo;
- procurement or partnership process;
- buyer-provided workflow/case;
- explicit commercial interest.

Generic autoresponses remain ROUTING, not qualified pipeline.

## Meeting conversion objective

For every substantive reply:
1. identify executive sponsor and technical/domain owner;
2. select one workflow with material economic consequence;
3. agree baseline and measurable success criteria;
4. agree paid-proof or post-proof commercial decision path;
5. run only authorized evidence-bound demonstration;
6. seek a signed commercial instrument if the proof clears the buyer's threshold.

## Capital-state vocabulary

- SENT: outreach transmitted.
- ROUTING: intermediary response/autoresponse.
- QUALIFIED: accountable buyer owner + material use case identified.
- MEETING: dated working session scheduled.
- POV-PROPOSED: paid proof scope presented.
- POV-SIGNED: paid proof contracted.
- ENTERPRISE-PROPOSED: enterprise/OEM commercial terms presented.
- LOI: signed non-binding/partially binding instrument as legally defined.
- CONTRACTED: executed binding contract value.
- INVOICED: invoice issued.
- CASH: funds collected.

Never collapse these states.
