Updated: 2026-09-27

## Objective

Distribute the existing MUSITU Axiom product through major AI ecosystems while preserving AXIOM ownership of the service and identifying a legitimate money flow for each channel.

## Commercial rules

1. Never represent distribution access as a payout agreement.
2. Never invent a foreign legal entity or payment profile to bypass regional eligibility.
3. Keep platform-specific payment/checkout tools out of ecosystems whose directory policies prohibit financial transactions.
4. Use platform marketplace/partner economics only where the platform's current terms explicitly support them.
5. Preserve AXIOM's independent billing service as the fallback monetization layer where platform policy permits linking or directing users to the service.

## Evidence anchors

- Anthropic plugin directory supports a single remote MCP connector and offers post-publication usage/discovery analytics.
- Anthropic directory policy excludes software that transfers money, cryptocurrency, financial assets, or executes financial transactions.
- Meta AI Connectors are currently developer preview; public publishing/discovery is later.
- Google Cloud Marketplace supports AI agents with subscription, usage-based, or combined pricing and monthly vendor payments; its current payout-region table does not list Zimbabwe.
- Google's Vendor Net Revenue Schedule specifies 97% for standard offers and eligible private offers below $1M TCV, subject to the schedule and agreement.

See the corresponding official platform sources in the project submission/research notes before making any external commercial representation.
