# MUSITU Axiom — Claude Marketplace Agent/Product Submission

Status: READY_FOR_COMMERCIAL_APPLICATION

## Commercial objective

List MUSITU Axiom as a Claude Marketplace agent/product so eligible enterprise customers can procure the product using a portion of their committed Anthropic spend.

Anthropic's Marketplace launched September 23, 2026 and explicitly supports:
- connectors and plugins;
- agents and products;
- service partners.

Anthropic states that teams can apply a portion of committed Anthropic spend to buy Claude-powered software from Marketplace partners.

## Product

**Name:** MUSITU Axiom

**Category:** Financial Services / Data & Analytics

**Product type:** Claude-powered quantitative intelligence and execution platform.

**Core value:** Axiom combines deterministic quantitative computation, verification, structured evidence, and governed workflow execution with Claude as one interchangeable intelligence provider.

## Enterprise use cases

- financial modeling and valuation;
- portfolio and risk analytics;
- statistical analysis;
- time-series forecasting;
- Monte Carlo scenario analysis;
- quantitative verification and cross-checking;
- evidence-backed research workflows;
- governed analytical workflow execution.

## Product architecture

Claude is a connected intelligence backend, not the owner of Axiom's control plane.

Axiom remains responsible for:
- task interpretation;
- quantitative operation selection;
- execution governance;
- evidence production;
- verification;
- recovery;
- artifact generation.

This preserves AXIOM's model-independent architecture while allowing Claude customers to consume the product through Anthropic's ecosystem.

## Commercial model to propose

Enterprise customer:
1. selects MUSITU Axiom from Claude Marketplace;
2. allocates eligible committed Anthropic spend to the product;
3. receives the Axiom product under the Marketplace commercial arrangement;
4. Anthropic handles the Marketplace procurement/billing flow;
5. MUSITU receives the applicable vendor settlement under the finalized Marketplace agreement.

**Important:** exact vendor settlement percentage, payout schedule, taxes, and settlement mechanics are not assumed here and must be confirmed in the Anthropic onboarding agreement.

## Product description

MUSITU Axiom is an evidence-native quantitative intelligence platform for enterprises. It combines Claude's frontier reasoning with a certified quantitative runtime for finance, statistics, optimization, time-series analysis, Monte Carlo scenarios, numerical computation, and independent calculation verification. Axiom governs the workflow, executes deterministic calculations, records structured evidence, and produces auditable results.

## Enterprise differentiation

Axiom is not another conversational wrapper around Claude.

Its product layer adds:
- deterministic computation;
- model-independent routing;
- verification;
- evidence artifacts;
- governed execution;
- recovery and replay;
- quantitative correctness controls.
