## Defensibility thesis
The durable moat is not a single model. It is the compound system of skill graph, evidence graph, temporal memory, causal models, simulation environments, evaluation corpus, proof ledger, governed permissions, and verified execution history.

## Public submission rule
Only workflows supported by the current public MCP surface or ordinary safe model reasoning belong in the OpenAI submission profile. Tool-dependent engineering, GUI, deployment, design and other private capabilities remain in the frontier profile until separately proven and approved.
