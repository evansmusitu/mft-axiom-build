# Enterprise Procurement Checklist

This checklist is an internal procurement execution aid and is **not a legally executed agreement**.

## Boundary
- Do not invent a price, discount, SLA, uptime/availability promise, legal approval, or customer signature.
- Do not collect or record payment card information.
- Negotiated price or SLA content is outside the runtime and requires an authorized human/legal/commercial process.

## Required evidence before entitlement activation
- [ ] Order form evidence hash recorded.
- [ ] Statement of work evidence hash recorded.
- [ ] Security appendix evidence hash recorded.
- [ ] Support process reference/evidence recorded.
- [ ] Privacy and data-handling evidence recorded.
- [ ] Authorized approval/execution evidence recorded.

## Activation rule
The enterprise contract remains `draft` until every required item is evidence-bound. Entitlement activation may occur only after the authorized execution transition. Suspending the contract must suspend the linked entitlement.

Missing evidence is a blocker, not an invitation to infer or fabricate commercial or legal state.
