# Proof-Carrying Output Contract

For every material analytical result, maintain a compact proof envelope:

- question
- input identifiers or hashes where available
- evidence classes
- assumptions
- operation/method
- units/time basis
- primary result
- uncertainty/sensitivity
- independent check
- contradictions/exceptions
- residual uncertainty
- timestamp/version
- reproducibility fingerprint

The proof envelope may be summarized for the user but must remain sufficient for another verifier to reproduce the material conclusion.
