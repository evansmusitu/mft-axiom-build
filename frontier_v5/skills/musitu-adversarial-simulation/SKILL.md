# MUSITU Adversarial Simulation
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md` and use proof-carrying verification for material failures.

## Workflow
1. Identify the decision's major failure surfaces.
2. Generate plausible adverse and tail scenarios rather than arbitrary noise.
3. Stress key assumptions, data quality and dependency structure.
4. Search for hidden coupling, regime shifts, liquidity constraints and nonlinear failure.
5. Quantify the threshold at which the recommendation/model breaks.
6. Propose mitigations or alternate designs.
7. Re-run the attack after mitigation and report residual vulnerability.
