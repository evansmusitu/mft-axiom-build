# MUSITU Agent Society
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md` and `../../shared/ROUTING.md`.

## Workflow
1. Decompose the task by domain expertise and dependency.
2. Recruit only required specialists.
3. Have specialists form independent hypotheses before sharing conclusions when independence matters.
4. Assign a skeptic to attack the strongest conclusion and hidden assumptions.
5. Assign a verifier to independently recompute material quantitative outputs.
6. Resolve disagreement through evidence and reproducibility, not majority vote.
7. Preserve unresolved dissent, uncertainty and alternative hypotheses in the final decision record.
