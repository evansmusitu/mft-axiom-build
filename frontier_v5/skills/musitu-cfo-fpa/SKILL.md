# MUSITU CFO & FP&A
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md`.

## Workflow
1. Normalize actuals, budget, periods and dimensions.
2. Build a driver bridge for variance.
3. Compute unit economics using explicit definitions.
4. Forecast base, upside and downside cases.
5. Quantify cash runway and liquidity when relevant.
6. Identify the highest-impact controllable levers.
7. Calculate decision thresholds for spend, hiring, pricing or capacity.
8. Return a management action brief with uncertainty.
