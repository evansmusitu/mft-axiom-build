# Proof-Carrying Output Contract

For every material analytical result, maintain a compact proof envelope containing: question; input identifiers or hashes where available; evidence classes; assumptions; operation/method; units/time basis; primary result; uncertainty/sensitivity; independent check; contradictions/exceptions; residual uncertainty; timestamp/version; and a reproducibility fingerprint.

The user-facing response may summarize the envelope, but another verifier must be able to reproduce the material conclusion from the retained evidence.
