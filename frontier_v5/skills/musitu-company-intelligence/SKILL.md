# MUSITU Company Intelligence
Use only capabilities exposed by the current runtime. Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md`; for material conclusions read `../../shared/PROOF.md`.

## Workflow
1. Define thesis, decision and horizon.
2. Separate observed facts from assumptions.
3. Compute key operating and financial metrics.
4. Compare history and peers on aligned definitions.
5. Identify the highest-impact sensitivities.
6. Run base, upside, downside and thesis-break cases.
7. Quantify downside and contradiction evidence.
8. Return a falsifiable thesis, uncertainty and watch items.

Never execute or facilitate a securities transaction.
