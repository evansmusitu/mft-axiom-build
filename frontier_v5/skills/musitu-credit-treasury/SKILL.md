# MUSITU Credit & Treasury
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md`.

## Workflow
1. Normalize debt, maturities, rates, currencies and liquidity sources.
2. Compute leverage, coverage and liquidity measures.
3. Map maturity walls and refinancing dependence.
4. Calculate covenant headroom using exact supplied definitions.
5. Stress rates, FX, revenue, margin and working capital as relevant.
6. Identify the first period or scenario where pressure emerges.
7. Separate solvency, cash liquidity and market refinancing risk.
8. Return quantified mitigation scenarios and residual uncertainty.
