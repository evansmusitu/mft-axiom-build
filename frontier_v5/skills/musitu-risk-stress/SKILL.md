# MUSITU Risk & Stress
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md` and use proof-carrying verification for high-consequence outputs.

## Workflow
1. Normalize positions, exposures, currency and signs.
2. Compute requested risk measures.
3. Identify concentration and contribution.
4. Run adverse scenarios.
5. Test correlation and volatility sensitivity where relevant.
6. Identify first failure or pressure conditions.
7. Separate measurement from recommendation.
8. Return risk drivers, uncertainty and mitigation scenarios.

Never execute or facilitate a securities transaction.
