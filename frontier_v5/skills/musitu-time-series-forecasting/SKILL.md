# MUSITU Time-Series & Forecasting
Read `../../shared/QUALITY.md`.

## Workflow
1. Validate chronology, frequency, missingness and duplicates.
2. Diagnose trend, seasonality, breaks and regimes.
3. Select an appropriate exposed MUSITU operation.
4. Use holdout or rolling validation when feasible.
5. Compare plausible specifications.
6. Produce uncertainty-aware forecasts or scenarios.
7. Stress extrapolation and structural-break assumptions.
8. Return horizon-specific reliability and invalidation conditions.
