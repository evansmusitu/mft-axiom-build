# MUSITU Chemistry Rescue 2026 — 30-Day National Launch Calendar

Primary destination pattern:
`https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=<approved-source>`

The calendar starts only after the production Worker and evidence gates pass. Every day uses one clear CTA and one approved source class.

## Week 1 — Establish useful proof and the sharing loop

**Day 1 — Launch controlled organic baseline**  
Publish one founder/brand post explaining the free Rescue check. Use `src=direct`. Share teacher and ambassador kits only with known, relevant contacts. Record baseline Rescue visits/starts; do not buy traffic yet.

**Day 2 — Electrochemistry misconception**  
Post a short “common electrode-sign mistake” lesson. End with the Rescue CTA. Creator version uses `src=creator`; MUSITU-owned version uses `src=direct`.

**Day 3 — Teacher activation**  
Send the approved teacher WhatsApp copy to opted-in/known Chemistry teachers. Point to `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=wa_teacher`. Ask for usefulness feedback, not endorsement.

**Day 4 — Organic Chemistry challenge**  
Publish one reaction-pathway question and explanation. Use the short-video script format and `src=direct` or `src=creator`.

**Day 5 — Student share prompt**  
Re-share the product’s built-in “share with a classmate” mechanic. The product itself emits the canonical `src=wa_student` peer link; do not append personal identifiers.

**Day 6 — Parent usefulness post**  
Publish a parent-facing explanation: free to start, focus revision, Premium optional. Send parent copy only through legitimate parent/community channels.

**Day 7 — Week 1 evidence review**  
Compare aggregate Rescue visits, starts, shares, peer starts and Premium intent by approved source. Keep only creatives that produce meaningful learner action. No optimization from likes alone.

## Week 2 — Expand teacher, school and creator distribution

**Day 8 — Equilibrium micro-lesson**  
Post a Le Chatelier question with a worked explanation. One Rescue CTA.

**Day 9 — Teacher Rescue Kit push**  
Direct teachers to `/chemistry/rescue/teacher-kit`. Ask them to print/share only in classes or groups they legitimately manage.

**Day 10 — Creator wave 1**  
Brief a small number of relevant education creators using `creator-brief.md`. All disclosed creator traffic uses `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=creator`.

**Day 11 — School outreach wave 1**  
Send `school-outreach.txt` to a small, manually selected set of schools or Chemistry departments. Use `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=school`.

**Day 12 — Calculations under time pressure**  
Publish a stoichiometry or concentration mini-test. Make the value complete even if the learner never clicks.

**Day 13 — Student story format without invented results**  
Show a real product walkthrough or founder demonstration. If no verified user story exists, demonstrate the workflow rather than presenting a testimonial.

**Day 14 — Week 2 review**  
Rank teacher, school, creator and direct sources by Rescue starts and downstream engagement. Pause low-quality channels.

## Week 3 — Controlled paid amplification

**Day 15 — Paid-media readiness check**  
Paid activity starts only if production health is stable and at least two organic creatives show useful downstream action. Prepare Meta copy using `src=meta` and TikTok copy using `src=tiktok`.

**Day 16 — Meta test A**  
Launch the smallest approved test from `budget-operations.md` against one proven creative. Destination: `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=meta`.

**Day 17 — TikTok/short-form test A**  
Only if the creative naturally fits the platform and account is eligible. Destination: `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=tiktok`.

**Day 18 — Teacher wave 2**  
Use teacher feedback to improve explanation, not claims. Re-share teacher kit to new legitimate teacher contacts.

**Day 19 — Creator wave 2**  
Retain only creators who generate real Rescue starts or shares. Replace weak creatives rather than increasing spend.

**Day 20 — Exam-pressure lesson**  
Publish a 30–45 second “what breaks under time pressure?” Chemistry challenge with one Rescue CTA.

**Day 21 — Week 3 paid review**  
Compare cost per Rescue start and downstream intent, plus support friction and performance. Stop any campaign that produces low-quality clicks or material support problems.

## Week 4 — Scale what is proven, preserve trust

**Day 22 — Ambassador activation**  
Approve a small number of ambassadors who accept `ambassador-rules.md`. Their destination is `https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=ambassador`.

**Day 23 — School outreach wave 2**  
Follow up only where there was legitimate prior contact or a relevant public institutional channel. Do not repeatedly message non-responsive recipients.

**Day 24 — High-value topic video**  
Publish the best-performing educational format on another high-friction Chemistry topic.

**Day 25 — Parent confidence explainer**  
Explain free vs Premium, verified payment and licence boundaries in plain language. Do not pressure parents with fear-based exam claims.

**Day 26 — Paid-media test B**  
Shift spend only toward the source/creative combination with the strongest evidence. Do not exceed the approved campaign cap.

**Day 27 — Teacher proof-of-use session**  
Invite willing teachers to try the Rescue flow themselves and give product feedback. Participation is not an endorsement.

**Day 28 — Peer-share reinforcement**  
Ask learners who found the free check useful to use the built-in classmate share button. Never request access to their contacts.

**Day 29 — National evidence consolidation**  
Review 29 days of aggregate funnel metrics, support issues, field performance and Premium intent. Identify which channels deserve continuation after Day 30.

**Day 30 — Publish/retain/stop decision**  
Retain proven organic loops. Continue paid channels only if downstream evidence justifies spend. Retire weak copy, creators or ambassador placements. Produce a dated launch evidence summary without inflating adoption or revenue claims.

## Non-negotiable cadence rules

- One useful Chemistry idea before one CTA.
- One approved source code per placement.
- Never encode a student, teacher, school or ambassador identity in the URL.
- No automated contact harvesting.
- No repeated messaging to people who have not engaged.
- No paid scale before product-health and organic-funnel evidence.
- If checkout, licence, support or performance health regresses, pause amplification before investigating.
