# MUSITU Chemistry Rescue Ambassador Rules

## Purpose

Ambassadors help real A-Level Chemistry students discover the free MUSITU Chemistry Rescue check through genuine peer, class and school networks.

Ambassador link:
`https://payments.mftintelligence.com/chemistry/rescue?src=ambassador`

## Required conduct

1. Share MUSITU only where it is genuinely relevant.
2. Do not spam WhatsApp groups, strangers, scraped contacts or unrelated communities.
3. Do not use automated cold messaging or forced contact-book access.
4. Do not create fabricated accounts, fake referrals, self-referral farms or artificial activity.
5. Do not publish fake results, fake testimonials, guaranteed-pass claims or outcomes you did not personally experience.
6. Do not claim official ZIMSEC, school, teacher or government endorsement without real written authorization.
7. Do not hide a paid/gifted relationship if one exists.
8. Do not pressure a learner to purchase Premium; the Rescue campaign remains free to start.
9. Protect learner privacy. Do not collect or publish another student's private diagnostic information without their explicit choice.

## What counts as useful contribution

Recognition should favor verified downstream learner engagement: genuine Rescue visits, free starts and useful follow-through. Raw clicks, copied messages or repeated self-generated traffic are not evidence of impact.

## Initial rewards policy

The initial programme uses recognition and limited legitimate rewards. Any future cash or commission system requires a separately verified anti-fraud and terms layer before launch.
