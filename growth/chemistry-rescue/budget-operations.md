# MUSITU Chemistry Rescue 2026 — Paid Amplification Budget Operations

## Budget envelope

The approved experimental paid-media envelope is **US$200 to US$500 total**, not a mandatory spend target.

- US$200 = minimum disciplined test envelope.
- US$500 = absolute campaign ceiling for this launch cycle unless a later explicit budget decision changes it.
- Spend may stop below US$200 if evidence is weak or product/support health deteriorates.
- No channel is entitled to budget merely because it exists in the plan.

Paid media starts only after production and organic-funnel gates are green.

## Stage 0 — Zero-spend evidence

Before any paid media:
- verify `/chemistry/rescue` production health;
- verify Free download, plans, checkout presentation and entitlement boundaries;
- collect organic Rescue-start evidence from direct, teacher, creator or student-share sources;
- identify at least two creatives that produce meaningful downstream action rather than views only.

Budget: **US$0**.

## Stage 1 — Creative signal test

Maximum: **US$40–US$80** total.

Suggested structure:
- 2–4 creatives;
- one or two platforms at most;
- small daily caps;
- source-coded destinations (`src=meta` or `src=tiktok`).

Primary decision metric: cost per Rescue start with healthy downstream engagement.

Stop immediately if:
- clicks arrive but Rescue starts are materially weak;
- support/payment confusion rises;
- production health regresses;
- a creative creates misleading expectations;
- platform traffic quality is clearly poor.

## Stage 2 — Controlled winner expansion

Only winners from Stage 1 may advance.

Additional maximum: **US$80–US$170**.

Requirements:
- downstream Rescue engagement remains healthy;
- no product/security/performance regression;
- source attribution remains bounded and aggregate-only;
- winning creative remains accurate after review.

Do not broaden audience and increase budget simultaneously unless the experiment can still be interpreted.

## Stage 3 — National amplification

Only if Stages 1–2 show sustained evidence.

Additional maximum: enough to bring cumulative spend to **US$200–US$500**, never above US$500 in this launch envelope.

Preference order:
1. proven creative + proven audience/channel;
2. proven creator amplification;
3. teacher/school organic distribution remains free and is never replaced by paid impressions;
4. new speculative creative receives a new small test, not winner-scale budget.

## Daily spend controls

- Set platform daily caps before launch.
- Review spend and aggregate funnel outcomes at least once per active paid day.
- Do not automatically raise budgets from platform recommendations.
- Do not enable uncontrolled auto-expansion if it prevents source/creative interpretation.
- Use one approved source class per campaign destination.
- Record platform, creative ID/name, date, spend, impressions, clicks, Rescue starts and any available downstream aggregate intent.

## Economics to monitor

Useful operating measures:
- cost per Rescue visit;
- cost per Rescue start;
- Rescue-start rate from paid visit;
- peer-share rate after paid start;
- Premium-intent rate;
- checkout progression where aggregate evidence permits;
- support contacts per paid cohort window;
- verified revenue only after actual verified settlement evidence exists.

Do not optimize to cost per click alone.

## Stop-loss policy

Pause paid amplification before investigation when any of the following occurs:
- checkout or entitlement gate failure;
- production availability or severe Core Web Vitals regression;
- abnormal support/payment complaints;
- misleading or stale creative discovered;
- spend exceeds the configured cap;
- material discrepancy between platform traffic and MUSITU aggregate start evidence;
- no credible downstream action after a reasonable small-sample test.

## Scale rule

Scale only by evidence. A campaign may move from US$200 toward US$500 only when the incremental spend continues to produce useful learner action at acceptable quality. If marginal quality deteriorates, hold or reduce spend.

## Recordkeeping

Maintain a dated spend ledger with:
- channel;
- campaign/creative label;
- approved source class;
- planned cap;
- actual spend;
- aggregate Rescue visits/starts;
- downstream intent where available;
- stop/continue decision;
- decision rationale.

Marketing cannot override checkout, payment, licence or refund state.
