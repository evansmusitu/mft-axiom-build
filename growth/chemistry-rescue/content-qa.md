# MUSITU Chemistry Rescue 2026 — Content QA

Every public asset must pass this checklist before publication or paid amplification.

## 1. Product truth

- Free means the documented free Rescue/Free product entry only.
- Premium features, duration, seat scope and price must match the live server-authoritative catalog.
- Never say a download itself grants Premium access.
- Never imply payment is complete before independent verified settlement.
- Never publish a settlement-success story without actual verified settlement evidence.

## 2. Education claims

Allowed:
- “helps identify areas that need work”
- “structured Chemistry revision”
- “timed practice”
- “free to start”

Not allowed:
- promises of a specific grade, mark, pass outcome or ranking
- claims that MUSITU can predict a learner’s official exam result
- invented before/after improvements
- invented user, school or teacher adoption numbers

## 3. Institutional language

- Do not imply endorsement, approval, partnership or affiliation with ZIMSEC, a ministry, school, teacher, examination body or public authority unless documented authorization exists.
- Do not use school or institutional logos without permission.
- A teacher trying or sharing MUSITU does not automatically make them an endorser.

## 4. Learner safeguarding and privacy

- Never collect or publish a learner’s private result for marketing without valid authorization and appropriate safeguarding.
- Never request access to a learner’s contact book.
- No scraped or purchased phone lists.
- No unsolicited repeated student messaging.
- No identity, phone number, school name, Device ID, order reference, email or personal campaign code in Rescue attribution URLs.
- Use only the eight approved source classes.

## 5. Social proof

- Testimonials must come from real people who actually used the product and consented to the specific use.
- Do not script a testimonial for someone to present as their own experience.
- Creator demonstrations must be clearly demonstrations unless they are genuine personal experience.
- Paid, gifted or materially compensated creator relationships should be disclosed where applicable.

## 6. Creative quality

A publishable asset should:
- teach or demonstrate something useful before the CTA;
- be understandable without exaggerated claims;
- have one primary CTA;
- use one correct source-coded Rescue destination;
- state “free to start” accurately where relevant;
- avoid shame, panic or manipulative exam fear;
- work on a low-bandwidth mobile connection where practical.

## 7. Paid-media QA

Before spend:
- production route is healthy;
- exact Worker version is known;
- checkout and entitlement gates remain healthy;
- privacy and field telemetry contracts remain intact;
- organic evidence identifies at least one useful creative;
- daily and total spend caps are set;
- stop conditions are understood.

## 8. Stop-publication triggers

Immediately stop or correct a creative when:
- a factual Chemistry error is found;
- product pricing or feature scope is stale;
- it implies unsupported endorsement;
- it presents example data as a real learner result;
- it generates material user confusion about Free vs Premium;
- it causes abnormal support/payment friction;
- a platform or legal requirement makes the asset non-compliant.

## 9. Pre-publish sign-off

Record for each material campaign asset:
- asset name/version;
- channel;
- source class;
- destination URL;
- reviewer;
- date reviewed;
- claims checked;
- disclosure required/not required;
- approval or rejection reason.

The launch calendar is permission to execute only after the product deployment gate is separately green.
