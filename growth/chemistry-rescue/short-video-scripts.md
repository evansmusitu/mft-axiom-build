**Teach:** “The anode is where oxidation occurs. In a galvanic cell it is the negative electrode because electrons leave it and travel through the external circuit. Don’t memorize signs without asking whether the cell is galvanic or electrolytic.”

## Script 2 — Equilibrium mistake

**Hook:** “Adding a catalyst changes the equilibrium position — true or false?”

**Teach:** “False. A catalyst speeds both forward and reverse reactions. It helps equilibrium get established faster but does not change the equilibrium constant or final equilibrium composition at that temperature.”
