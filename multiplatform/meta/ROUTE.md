# Meta route

The comparative evaluation adapter targets the Meta Model API where account and product access are available.

Meta's official developer materials also document a WhatsApp Business Tools MCP for managing WhatsApp Business from AI agents. That is a Meta-provided Business Messaging capability, not evidence of a generic public third-party MCP submission directory.

This lane therefore prepares interoperability and evaluation without inventing a Meta submission mechanism. Any future WhatsApp Business integration should use a separate Meta App/development configuration and the applicable OAuth, webhook, and review requirements.
