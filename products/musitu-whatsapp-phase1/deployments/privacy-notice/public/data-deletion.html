Data deletion instructions

Evans Musitu operates this private development and staging service. To request deletion of information handled by MUSITU WhatsApp AI Employee, email evansmusitu5@gmail.com with the subject MUSITU data deletion request.

1. Describe the request

Identify the business you contacted or worked with, the account or WhatsApp number involved, approximate dates, and the records you want reviewed. Share only the details necessary to locate the records. You can first describe the request without sending a full conversation history.

2. Verify ownership

We may ask you to confirm control of the relevant account or number, or coordinate with the relevant business. We use a proportionate check before disclosing or changing personal records. Never send a password, authenticator code, API key, payment credential or identity document with the initial request.

3. Receive a scope and outcome response

Requests are handled manually through the privacy email. We review relevant message and recipient records, business or booking records, linked approvals and usage records, identity records and recovery copies as applicable. We reply by email with the request scope, any necessary clarification, the outcome, and any records retained and the reason. You can reply in the same email thread for status or to raise a concern.

Removal may require coordination with the relevant business or a service provider. Information needed for security, legal obligations, dispute handling or preventing unwanted contact may need to be retained. Recovery copies are reviewed separately; removing a live record does not immediately erase every backup. Where deletion cannot be completed, we explain the limitation and available next steps.

Current service limits

This staging service has no self-service deletion tool or automatic deletion callback. Its 30-day message visibility limit is not a guarantee of physical deletion. We do not promise an instant deletion time; requests are reviewed under applicable requirements.

STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE requests suppression of replies and does not itself erase existing records. If you want both suppression and deletion review, state both in your email. MUSITU cannot delete your WhatsApp account or independently controlled copies held by another business or provider.

Read the privacy notice for information categories, purposes, providers and retention. Privacy contact: evansmusitu5@gmail.com.
