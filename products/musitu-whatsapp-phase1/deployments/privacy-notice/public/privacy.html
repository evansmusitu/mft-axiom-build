Privacy notice

This notice covers MUSITU WhatsApp AI Employee, operated by Evans Musitu. It describes the current private development and staging service for business inquiries, appointments and staff-assisted customer service. The service is undergoing qualification and is not open for public customer use.

For privacy questions or requests, contact evansmusitu5@gmail.com. For deletion, follow our data deletion instructions.

Information we process

Depending on enabled features, records include business account and staff identity details; staff memberships and roles; business knowledge and appointment resources; bookings; WhatsApp account, phone and message identifiers; conversation text and recipient details; consent and opt-out records; response approvals; security and operational audit events; and AI usage records. Current acceptance work uses synthetic business records and an owner-controlled development recipient. Customer imports are not enabled.

When you visit this notice site, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, time, requested URL and browser information to deliver and protect the site. This notice site has no application login, contact form, advertising tracker or analytics script.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate staff, apply business-specific access, handle inquiries and human handover, manage appointments and conflicts, prepare approved responses, respect opt-outs, and investigate operational or security issues. Access depends on business membership and role. The service is not used to sell personal information or deliver targeted advertising.

AI assistance and human decisions

Where the business owner permits AI assistance and staff request it, the selected message question and approved FAQ question choices can be sent to Groq for matching. Answers are selected from approved business knowledge stored in MUSITU. Responses require human approval; AI assistance does not independently send messages or execute tools. Do not include passwords, financial credentials, authenticator codes or other sensitive secrets in messages.

Service providers and business responsibilities

The current service uses Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for its development channel, Vercel for hosting, Neon for database storage, ZITADEL for identity, Resend for authentication email and Groq for AI inference. These providers process information needed for their respective services. Provider processing may take place in countries different from yours.

Where a business chooses the purpose and content of a conversation, that business is responsible for its own use of the information. You can contact the relevant business as well as MUSITU. We will coordinate a request where it concerns records handled for that business. Provider-held WhatsApp, identity or email records may also be subject to the provider's separate policies and controls.

Storage and security

The staging service uses access restrictions, encrypted message and recipient content, encrypted server-side session payloads, restricted database roles and business isolation controls. No system can guarantee complete security. These measures are not a claim of certification or completed production qualification.

Retention

Message records currently have a 30-day application visibility limit. Expired content is excluded from normal application reads; this does not mean every underlying record or backup is automatically erased after 30 days. Other business, booking, consent, approval, audit, identity and usage records, and recovery copies, may be retained separately. Automated deletion and a complete production retention schedule are not yet operational.

For a privacy request, we review the records in scope and explain what can be removed, what must be retained and why, and how recovery copies are affected. We retain information only as needed for the service, security, opt-out protection, recovery or applicable obligations. Deletion is not promised to be instantaneous or automatic.

Your choices and requests

You may ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction or an objection concerning your information. Available rights and response requirements depend on applicable law. Email the privacy contact with the relevant business and a short description of your request. We may ask for a proportionate ownership check before disclosing or changing records. Do not send passwords, authenticator codes, API keys or identity documents with your initial request.

STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE is intended to suppress further replies in the messaging workflow. An opt-out is separate from erasure of existing records. Use the deletion instructions when you want records reviewed for removal.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice when the service or its processing changes. The effective date above identifies this version. Contact Evans Musitu at evansmusitu5@gmail.com with questions or concerns.
