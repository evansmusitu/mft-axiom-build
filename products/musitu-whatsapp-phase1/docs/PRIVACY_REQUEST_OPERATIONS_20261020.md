# Privacy request handling — private staging

Operator and responsible custodian: Evans Musitu. Public intake:
evansmusitu5@gmail.com. Approved by user on 20 October 2026. These are manual
instructions, not evidence of a delivered request, verified mailbox monitoring,
successful erasure or a completed legal review.

1. Operator reviews privacy email and records request received date, scoped
   business, requested right and a private reference. Do not copy message bodies,
   phone numbers, identity evidence or request details into Git/public logs.
2. Establish minimal account/number ownership; do not ask for passwords, OTPs,
   seeds or API keys. Do not disclose another tenant's records. Coordinate with
   the relevant business where it determines purpose/content.
3. Inventory linked records and processors before modifying anything. Consider
   message/contact content and identifiers, bookings, approvals, suppression,
   audit, model usage, identity and recovery copies. Preserve tenant isolation
   and referential integrity. A request is not blanket authorization to delete.
4. Document lawful retention/processing grounds and applicable response deadline
   for this request. No invented universal deadline or compliance assurance.
5. Prepare scoped, reviewable export/correction/deletion actions with recovery
   implications. Obtain required specific authorization before irreversible
   changes. Existing workflows are not yet qualified; do not execute a generic
   cascade or delete suppression records in a way that re-enables contact.
6. Reply through the same private email thread with scope, status, limitations,
   any retained classes/reasons, backup treatment and outcome. Outgoing email
   requires explicit user instruction under this session's messaging boundary.
7. Keep a private minimal disposition record. Before any restore, reconcile
   subsequent privacy actions and suppressions; do not restore erased content
   into normal use without accounting for the request.

No request email was sent, mailbox rule/automation created, or customer record
changed by establishing this runbook. Deletion qualification, full retention
schedule, provider/transfer arrangements and intake monitoring remain release
requirements. The public instructions accurately disclose manual handling and
lack of instant/automatic erasure.
