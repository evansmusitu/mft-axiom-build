## Primary-law checks that remain material

1. **Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07], sections 15–16.** Where applicable personal data is obtained directly or indirectly, the Act requires disclosure of the controller's name and address together with processing purposes and supporting fair-processing information. The current public copy names MUSITU and supplies the support email, but it does not publish a controller address.
   Primary source: https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng%402022-03-11

2. **Companies and Other Business Entities Act [Chapter 24:31], section 28.** A registered business entity must use its name in its business papers / official publications. Whether the MUSITU brand as currently displayed is the legally sufficient registered-business name for this website cannot be established from public-safe release evidence without exposing or independently reviewing the relevant entity particulars.
   Primary source: https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2019/4/eng%402020-10-28

3. **Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44], section 52.** The detailed electronic-transaction supplier particulars apply when goods or services are offered for sale, hire or exchange by electronic transaction. The corporate site candidate expressly does not complete such a transaction; any linked destination that does must satisfy its own applicable transaction disclosures.
   Primary source: https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2019/5/eng%402019-12-10
