## Ownership and triage

The MUSITU website operator owns the queue. Internal handling is role-based; public pages do not need to identify individual staff.

1. **Security or suspected compromise:** acknowledge as soon as practicable; preserve evidence; avoid requesting secrets; escalate immediately to the website/infrastructure owner.
2. **Privacy/data-rights request:** acknowledge within 2 business days where practicable; verify identity using the minimum information necessary; route to the privacy/operator decision-maker; record the final disposition.
3. **Accessibility issue:** acknowledge within 2 business days where practicable; reproduce on the affected route/device/assistive technology where possible; prioritize blockers to core navigation and contact.
4. **General product/corporate enquiry:** target an initial response within 5 business days where practicable.
5. **Spam/abuse:** no response obligation; preserve only what is reasonably needed for security/abuse handling.

These are internal service objectives and do not create a public service-level agreement.

## Privacy and security handling

- Do not request passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, medical records or unrelated sensitive material through ordinary support email.
- Use correspondence only for the enquiry, operational follow-up, security, legal obligations and defensible recordkeeping.
- Restrict mailbox access to authorized operators.
- Do not use enquiry addresses for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis/permission.
- Escalate any suspected data incident before deleting evidence.
- Retention must follow the final approved operational retention schedule and applicable legal duties; this document does not invent a fixed statutory period.
