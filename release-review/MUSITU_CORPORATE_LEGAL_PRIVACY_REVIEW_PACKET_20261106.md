## Zimbabwe legal points requiring qualified reviewer confirmation

### 1. Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]
Sections 13–15 require lawful, fair and transparent processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy/retention discipline, data-subject rights, and disclosures when data is obtained directly. Section 15 includes the controller's name and address, processing purposes, direct-marketing objection information where relevant, and supporting recipient/response information.

**Reviewer decision required:** Does the current corporate website/support-email collection require publication of a physical/public controller address? If yes, specify the minimum legally sufficient public address that can be used without exposing unrelated private registration records.

### 2. SI 155 of 2024 / POTRAZ data-controller obligations
The Regulations and POTRAZ guidance establish licensing/registration, DPO and notification obligations for controllers subject to them, including notification of certain processing and cross-border transfers.

**Reviewer decision required:** Confirm which licensing/registration/DPO obligations apply to this operator and whether any pre-publication regulatory action must occur before the website begins processing support correspondence or hosting/security logs.

### 3. Cross-border processing
The candidate notice currently states that infrastructure and communication providers may process information in more than one jurisdiction. POTRAZ guidance requires assessment/safeguards and documentation for relevant cross-border transfers.

**Reviewer decision required:** Confirm the lawful transfer mechanism and whether the public notice needs more specificity for Cloudflare, Google-hosted mailbox services and Resend.

### 4. Children
The corporate website does not require visitor accounts and should not solicit sensitive data from children. Some linked education products may be used by minors and require their own product-specific notices and guardian-consent controls where applicable.

**Reviewer decision required:** Confirm whether the corporate notice should add an explicit children/minors section for unsolicited support correspondence.

### 5. Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44]
Part IV, including section 52, contains online disclosure requirements where a supplier offers goods or services for sale, hire or exchange through an electronic transaction. Those disclosures can include legal status, physical address, telephone, registration information and place for legal service. The current corporate website expressly does **not** itself create a paid service agreement; separate services can have separate transaction disclosures.

**Reviewer decision required:** Confirm whether the corporate information site is outside section 52 because it is not the transactional offer surface, and identify which linked transactional surfaces require the statutory disclosures. If section 52 applies to this site, identify the minimum mandatory public particulars.

### 6. Website Terms
The candidate terms preserve mandatory consumer/data-protection rights, avoid guarantees of returns/grades/availability, distinguish prototypes from releases, and do not purport to waive gross negligence or mandatory rights.

**Reviewer decision required:** Approve or mark changes for governing-law/jurisdiction language, warranty/availability wording, intellectual-property language and any consumer-facing disclosures.

### 7. Brand / trademark boundary
The owner has approved the exact Axis Knot master mark for website publication. The source visual-system document states that trademark-similarity clearance remains required before final registration.

**Reviewer decision required:** Confirm whether publication before trademark registration is acceptable and whether a targeted trademark search is recommended or required before launch.
